Redesign the Vigil Pro paywall screen to feel more polished, premium, and mobile-first.

Main goals
The paywall should look much better visually.
The yearly plan must appear first and be selected by default.
Before the pricing options, add a short “preparing for you” / onboarding-style section so the screen feels warmer and more premium.
Keep the subscription logic intact — this is mainly a UI/UX redesign.

Clean top bar with back button.

headline like: “We’re preparing everything for you now”
short supportive text like: “Vigil Pro unlocks your full experience and gives you smarter tools to track, understand, and improve your spending.”
Under that, show 3 short benefit rows/cards, for example:
Voice capture for expenses
Receipt & bank-message scanning
Deeper AI analysis and smart categories
2. Main paywall section
Then show a strong headline, e.g.:
“Unlock Vigil Pro”
or “Unlock everything Vigil can do for you”
Optional short subheadline explaining value.
3. Pricing options
Show Yearly option first.
Show Monthly option second.
The Yearly option should be highlighted as the recommended/best value choice.
The yearly card should clearly show:
plan name
savings message (example: “Best value” or “Save more”)
annual billing note
localized price if available
The monthly card should be visually secondary.
4. CTA
One strong primary button at the bottom.
CTA text should update based on selected plan, for example:
“Continue with Yearly”
“Continue with Monthly”
5. Footer

Minimal, premium, elegant, and clean.
White or very light background.

Better spacing, hierarchy, and card styling than the current screen.
